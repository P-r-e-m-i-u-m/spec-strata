LLMs are governed in layers. Most comparisons ignore that.

What the data shows

Five findings from coding the corpus

01

Labs diverge in artifact type, not just content

Anthropic's primary artifact sits at Tier 1, OpenAI's at Tier 2, xAI's at Tier 3 — and Google has published none. Most "prompt comparisons" compare different kinds of documents without saying so.

02

Convergent structure, divergent philosophy

Anthropic and OpenAI both use a ranked authority hierarchy — but justify it completely differently: developer steerability vs. reasoning the model should internalize.

03

Transparency can be reactive, not proactive

xAI's prompt-publishing commitment followed a 2025 incident — a different category from Anthropic's and OpenAI's proactive framing, with different implications for trust.

04

Product category predicts instruction emphasis

Coding-agent products emphasize tool-orchestration governance over content-safety — consistent with safety already living in a training-time layer.

05

"Living document" claims are sometimes verifiable

Anthropic's deployed system prompt has a public, dated changelog: 18 revisions over ~23 months — an existence proof, not just rhetoric.
